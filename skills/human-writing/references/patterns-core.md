# Pattern taxonomy — 15 categories, all heuristic

Rules find candidates. Density and context decide what is worth fixing.
One occurrence of anything below is not a defect; a cluster inside one
paragraph usually is. Never claim an author from patterns.

| # | Category | What it costs the reader | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Formulaic structure | Identical paragraph shapes, predictable intros/closers | Merge, reorder, let one paragraph run long |
| 2 | Artificial contrast | "not X, but Y" doing weight instead of content | State Y directly when Y is the point |
| 3 | Rule of three | Triads as wallpaper | Keep if the three items are the set |
| 4 | Signposting | Narrating the text's own structure | Delete "Im Folgenden"/"Let's dive into" |
| 5 | Promotional inflation | Booster adjectives without checkable claims | Replace with the number/mechanism or cut |
| 6 | Empty conclusions | Final paragraph = echo | End on the last real fact |
| 7 | Synthetic enthusiasm | Unmotivated excitement | Excite once, where earned, or not |
| 8 | Repetitive rhythm | Same sentence length forever | Split one, merge two |
| 9 | Generic transitions | Moreover/however as glue | Rely on content order |
| 10 | Pseudo-profundity | Deep-sounding, says nothing | Say the concrete thing or nothing |
| 11 | Excessive hedging | Stack of qualifiers hiding a claim | Commit or cite |
| 12 | Synonym cycling | Three names for one thing | One term, used consistently |
| 13 | Formatting overuse | Bold/bullets where sentences belong | Prose unless structure IS the content |
| 14 | Overexplaining | Saying it twice in different clothes | Once, clearly |
| 15 | Emotional overstatement | Small facts get big feelings | Match feeling to fact |

## Always allowed (do not "fix" these)

Humor, irony, colloquial voice, emotion, deliberate repetition, technical
vocabulary, precise terminology, literary devices, uneven sentence lengths,
personal quirks, blunt statements, unusual-but-fit phrasing, a single em
dash, short sentences, lists, metaphors. Creative writing follows
`creative-writing.md`, not this table.

## Severity ladder

informational (density) → editorial (context) → binding (cluster/meaning
damage). Only meaning damage blocks delivery.
