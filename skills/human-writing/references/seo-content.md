# SEO for humans first

Google Search Central: helpful, people-first content. This skill writes
for the searcher, not the crawler; SEO checks are structural observations,
never auto-edited without approval.

## Checks (reported, then proposed)

- Title: describes the page's actual content, 10–70 chars, no keyword chains.
- Meta description: 50–160 chars, specific to the page, no "In this page we…"
- H1: exactly one, names the subject; h2s follow content, not SEO template.
- Internal links: anchor text names the target ("installation guide", never
  "click here" / "hier").
- Topic naming: same concept, same term site-wide (drift breaks both trust
  and internal relevance).
- Information structure: answer early; sections genuinely needed.

## Forbidden edits without explicit scope

URLs, slugs, canonicals, structured data, redirects, hreflang. Metadata only
inside the approved change set.

## Anti-patterns (flag)

Keyword stuffing (density ≠ ranking signal past the point of sense), filler
to reach word counts, interchangeable per-page meta boilerplate, unsupported
superlatives, template intros that never mention the page's subject.
Good SEO copy here = the specific, checkable sentence the reader came for,
phrased in the words the reader used.
