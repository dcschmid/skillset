# English — Before / After

## 1. Landing intro

**Before**
> In today's fast-paced digital world, it's important to note that our
> seamless, innovative platform empowers teams to unlock their potential
> and take their workflows to the next level.

**After**
> The converter runs 3.5 MB/s on a 2019 laptop. No server, no cloud.

Booster stack → the checkable claim. Missing facts reported, never invented.

## 2. Contrast frame + closer

**Before**
> It's not just a tool. It's a movement. The future isn't coming — it's
> already here. Let that sink in.

**After**
> Six people ship it weekly. Last release: 80 ms parse time.

Dramatic fragments and faux-profundity replaced by facts sized to the page.

## 3. Left alone

"The parser halts when input is empty." — plain, exact, no edit. Isolated
hits (one dash, one short sentence) are not findings.
